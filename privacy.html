Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

About StudySpace

StudySpace is an independent educational website for students. It does not require an account and does not ask students to submit their name, student ID, school login or other personal details to use the study notes and quiz features. The site is not an official school or UPMSP website.

Advertising and third-party technologies

StudySpace plans to use Google AdSense to display advertisements after the site is configured in an AdSense account. When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to deliver, limit, measure and help prevent invalid activity on advertisements. These technologies may process information about a browser or visit. Advertising and data use are governed by the relevant third-party policies.

For details, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. Ad personalisation settings may be available through Google My Ad Center, depending on account, region and settings.

Cookies and choices

Google or other third-party vendors may place or read cookies in a browser when advertising features are enabled. Users can manage cookies through their browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some advertising features. Where consent or a certified consent management platform is required by applicable law or Google policy, the site owner must configure the appropriate consent settings in the advertising account before serving ads in those regions.

Data handled by StudySpace

The site’s class, subject and chapter selectors and quiz are designed to run in the browser. StudySpace does not currently provide user accounts, a server-side learning profile or a form for sending personal study notes. Third-party services such as the hosting provider and advertising provider may process technical request data under their own policies.

Children and students

StudySpace is an educational resource for secondary and senior-secondary students. It is not intended to request personal information from children. Advertising settings must be configured to comply with Google policies and applicable privacy laws, including any requirements that apply when content or visitors are child-directed. Do not submit personal or sensitive information through this website.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated when site features or advertising settings change. StudySpace is a small independent project; a public contact address has not yet been designated on this page.
